The warm-up is done standing, and its last move prepares the workout's first exercise. The cool-down goes from standing down to lying and needs no wall; every change of side in it pauses for 4 seconds, so the second side gets its full 15. Inside a running block, "Skip warm-up" and "Skip cool-down" ask before they end it. A first workout is about 31 minutes with the warm-up and cool-down.
